Refund Policy

Our refund policy for onsite printer services.

This Refund Policy explains when refunds may be available for services provided by ProPrinterServices. Please review this policy before scheduling an onsite visit.

Service-Based Business

ProPrinterServices provides onsite printer services rather than physical products. Our charges cover the technician's scheduled visit and the services performed at your location.

Appointment Cancellation

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please contact us as early as possible. Requests made before the scheduled visit may be eligible for cancellation without additional charges, depending on the circumstances.

Refund Eligibility

Refund requests are reviewed individually. If a payment was made for a service that could not be provided due to circumstances within our control, we will review the situation and determine whether a full or partial refund is appropriate.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are generally not available once an onsite visit has been completed or when services have been performed as requested. We also cannot provide refunds for issues caused by hardware failure, manufacturer defects, or third-party products.

Processing Approved Refunds

When a refund is approved, it will be processed using the original payment method whenever possible. Processing times may vary depending on your payment provider or financial institution.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Refund Policy or wish to request a refund, please contact ProPrinterServices through our Contact page. We will review your request and respond as soon as possible.
