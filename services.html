Services

Every visit, laid out plainly.

Below is what each service covers, so you know exactly what to expect before you book. Final pricing depends on your printer model and setup, and we always confirm it with you first.

Printer setup

Unboxing a new printer, attaching cables or connecting wirelessly, loading ink or toner, and running an initial test print to confirm it's working.

Typical visit: 30–45 min Request this

Installation on your devices

Installing printer drivers and software on your computer, laptop, or additional devices so they can send print jobs directly.

Typical visit: 30–60 min Request this

Network & Wi-Fi configuration

Connecting your printer to your home or office Wi-Fi network so every authorized device on the network can print to it.

Typical visit: 30–45 min Request this

Moving or reconnecting a printer

Reconnecting an existing printer after a move, a new router, or a new computer — including re-adding it to every device that needs it.

Typical visit: 30–45 min Request this

Maintenance & cleaning

Print head cleaning, alignment, roller cleaning, and general upkeep to help your printer produce clean, consistent prints.

Typical visit: 30–45 min Request this

Ink & toner guidance

Help identifying the correct ink or toner for your model, and installing cartridges correctly.

Typical visit: 15–30 min Request this

How pricing works

Pricing depends on the service, your printer model, and your location. We give you a price after hearing what you need, and confirm it with you before scheduling — you'll never be charged a surprise amount at your door.

  • Quotes are given before you commit to booking a visit.
  • The price we confirm is the price you pay, barring extra work you approve on the spot.
  • If we determine your issue isn't something a visit can resolve, we'll tell you before charging anything.

Not sure which service you need?

Describe your printer and what's going on, and we'll tell you which visit fits — no obligation to book.
