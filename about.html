About us

Printer help, done in person, by people who show up.

ProPrinterServices is a local, in-person printer service. We started this business because printer setup shouldn't mean an hour on the phone with someone reading from a script.

How we work

Every job we take on is handled the same way: we ask what you need, quote a price, schedule a time, and send a technician to your address. There's no call queue and no software to install on your end before we arrive.

We work with home offices, small businesses, and households — anywhere a physical visit makes more sense than trying to explain a printer setting over the phone.

Who it's for

People setting up a new printer, moving to a new home or office, switching to a new Wi-Fi network, or whose printer has simply stopped cooperating with their computer.

If you'd rather have someone handle it in person than spend an afternoon on it yourself, that's what we're here for.

Transparency

What we don't do

Printer service ads have a reputation problem, and we'd rather be upfront about where we draw the line than leave you guessing.

  • We don't ask for remote access to your computer, at any point.
  • We don't cold-call people or run pop-up warnings telling you to call us.
  • We don't charge before agreeing on a price with you first.
  • We don't pressure you to buy extended plans, extra hardware, or software you didn't ask for.
  • We do show up, do the work in front of you, and leave once it's tested and working.

Where we work

Homes

Home printer setups, Wi-Fi connections, and multi-device printing for the household.

Small offices

Single-printer or small multi-printer setups for offices with a handful of desks.

Local visits only

We currently serve the greater metro area. Check with us before booking if you're unsure whether you're in range.

Have a question before you book?

Reach out and we'll answer honestly — including telling you if a visit isn't the right fit for your issue.
